SUBJECT: PROMULGATION OF UNIFIED TARIFF ARCHITECTURE FOR THE NATIONAL NATURAL GAS GRID (AMENDMENT NO. 3/2026)

In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 11 and Section 22 of the Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board Act, 2006 (19 of 2006):

1. The Board hereby notifies the operationalization of the rationalized Unified Pipeline Tariff Mechanism across interconnected natural gas transmission systems nationwide (including Hazira-Vijaipur-Jagdishpur, Urja Ganga Jagdishpur-Haldia-Bokaro-Dhamra, and Dahej-Uran-Panvel networks).

2. KEY DETERMINATIONS:
   (a) Zone 1 Tariff (Distance <= 300 km from source/entry point): ₹42.80 per MMBTU (Gross Calorific Value basis).
   (b) Zone 2 Tariff (Distance > 300 km into hinterland/Eastern India): Harmonized at ₹68.40 per MMBTU, representing a 28% reduction in long-distance transportation costs to accelerate industrial gas penetration in non-coastal states.
   (c) Administered Gas Transmission Priority: Certified domestic APM gas supplied under MoPNG quota allocations to City Gas Distribution (CNG-transport and PNG-domestic) and fertilizer manufacturing shall be accorded non-discriminatory open-access transmission at Levelized Tariffs.

3. PPAC is directed to incorporate these revised transmission ceilings in the Monthly Statutory Digest and Ready Reckoner publications.
