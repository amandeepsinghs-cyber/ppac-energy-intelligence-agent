[PARLIAMENT OF INDIA & REGULATORY WATCH DISPATCH]
OFFICIAL ENERGY POLICY, PARLIAMENTARY SUBMISSIONS & GLOBAL INTELLIGENCE DIGEST

1. PARLIAMENTARY SCRUTINY (LOK SABHA & RAJYA SABHA Q&A):
- Lok Sabha Starred Question No. 142 (Answered 12 Aug 2026): Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas confirmed that OMC under-recoveries on domestic LPG were fully amortized through targeted fiscal provisions, with ₹12,000 Cr allocated for PMUY (Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana) DBT subsidies.
- Parliamentary Standing Committee on Petroleum (32nd Report): Tabled recommendations emphasizing accelerated crude storage filling at Strategic Petroleum Reserves (SPR) Phase-II caverns in Chandikhol (Odisha) and Padur (Karnataka) to insulate against tanker disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz.
- Rajya Sabha Unstarred Question No. 984: Ethanol blending in petrol (E20) reached a national average of 18.6% across 24 States, achieving foreign exchange savings of over ₹28,500 Cr in the current fiscal cycle.

2. STATUTORY REGULATORY ORDERS (PNGRB & DGH GAZETTE):
- Petroleum & Natural Gas Regulatory Board (PNGRB) issued Public Notice 45/2026 notifying revised unified pipeline tariffs across the National Gas Grid, equalizing transmission tariffs across Zone 1 (<300 km) and Zone 2 (>300 km) to incentivize industrial natural gas adoption in Eastern India.
- Directorate General of Hydrocarbons (DGH) formally opened bids for 28 exploration blocks under Open Acreage Licensing Policy (OALP) Bid Round X, covering deepwater sedimentary basins in the Mahanadi and Andaman offshore tracts.

3. GLOBAL BENCHMARK WATCH (EIA & IEA OUTLOOK):
- U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA STEO): Forecasts global liquid fuels consumption to average 104.2 million bpd in late 2026, driven predominantly by Asian non-OECD transportation demand.
- International Energy Agency (IEA Oil Market Report): Revised Indian oil demand growth projection upward to +240 kb/d YoY, highlighting India as the world's primary global demand growth engine through 2030.
