[MINISTRY OF PETROLEUM & NATURAL GAS - ECONOMIC ADVISORY DIVISION]
MONTHLY POLICY & MARKET ENVIRONMENT DISPATCH - AUGUST 2026

1. GLOBAL CRUDE ENVIRONMENT & OPEC+ DEVELOPMENTS:
- OPEC+ ministerial monitoring committee confirmed adherence to voluntary output adjustments of 2.2 million bpd through Q3 2026, sustaining Brent physical benchmark premiums.
- Middle East sour benchmark (Oman/Dubai) traded at an average discount of $3.60/bbl relative to North Sea Dated Brent, reflecting sustained regional freight and tanker risk premiums.
- Indian refiners maintained an optimal 75.6% sour / 24.4% sweet crude intake ratio to maximize diesel and jet fuel yields while capturing gross refining margins (GRM) above $8.20/bbl.

2. DOMESTIC NATURAL GAS & APM PRICE REVISION:
- In accordance with the statutory Kirit Parikh committee guidelines notified by MoPNG, the domestic APM natural gas price remains capped at the statutory ceiling of $6.50/MMBTU (Gross Calorific Value basis), shielding city gas distribution (CGD) and fertilizer sectors from international LNG volatility.
- HPHT (High Pressure High Temperature) deepwater gas ceiling benchmark notified at $9.87/MMBTU for the ongoing pricing half-year, incentivizing domestic production in KG-DWN-98/2 and Reliance-bp deepwater blocks.

3. DOMESTIC DEMAND DYNAMICS & MONSOON PROGRESSION:
- Monsoon precipitation recorded at 104% of Long Period Average (LPA) during August, leading to seasonal moderation in agricultural diesel consumption for pump-sets across Northern agrarian corridors.
- Commercial aviation turbine fuel (ATF) exhibited robust 8.4% YoY expansion driven by post-pandemic record passenger throughput at metro terminals (DEL, BOM, BLR).
- BPCL reported a provisional anomaly in rural LPG off-take (-30.0% MoM) attributable to local distributor ERP database migration across 4 state circles. Data reconciliation underway under Section 0 audit protocols.
