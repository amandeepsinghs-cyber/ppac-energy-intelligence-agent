OFFICE MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT: BUDGETARY PROVISIONING & DBT RELEASE AUTHORIZATION FOR PRADHAN MANTRI UJJWALA YOJANA (PMUY) DOMESTIC LPG SUBSIDIES

1. The undersigned is directed to refer to the Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas (MoPNG) D.O. Letter No. P-20012/2/2026-PPAC dated 18 July 2026 regarding fiscal allocation for targeted domestic LPG subsidies under Direct Benefit Transfer for LPG (DBTL / PAHAL) and PMUY beneficiary households.

2. In accordance with the decision of the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA), the Department of Expenditure hereby conveys formal financial concurrence for the release of ₹12,000 Crore (Twelve Thousand Crore Rupees) for the ongoing fiscal period, earmarked specifically for:
   (a) Direct subsidy credit of ₹300 per 14.2 kg cylinder for up to 12 refills per annum across 10.41 Crore PMUY registered families;
   (b) Advance festive allocation of ₹640 Crore to OMC settlement accounts (IOCL, BPCL, HPCL) to buffer anticipated post-monsoon consumption increases; and
   (c) Full amortization of prior-period operational carry-over balances.

3. MoPNG is requested to ensure that monthly volume certifications are reconciled through the PPAC Autonomous Verification Architecture prior to secondary tranche drawdowns.
