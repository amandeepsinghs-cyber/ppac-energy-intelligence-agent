SUBJECT: ADMITTED LOK SABHA QUESTIONS - STATISTICAL BRIEFS REQUIRED (STARRED Q. 1875 & UNSTARRED Q. 3165)

Sir,
I am directed to convey that the Hon'ble Speaker, Lok Sabha, has admitted the following questions for oral/written answers during the upcoming Parliamentary Session:

1. STARRED QUESTION NO. 1875:
   (a) Whether the Ministry has monitored the impact of recent crude price fluctuations on the Under-Recoveries of Public Sector OMCs (IOCL, BPCL, HPCL);
   (b) The quantum of retail price stabilization support extended to consumers; and
   (c) State-wise retail selling price buildup and duty realization details for Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, and Kolkata.

2. UNSTARRED QUESTION NO. 3165:
   (a) National progress under the Ethanol Blended Petrol (EBP) Programme, specifically verification of achieving the 19.05% blending threshold towards the 20% (E20) mandate;
   (b) Net foreign exchange outlays conserved through indigenous biofuel blending (estimated at over ₹28,500 Cr); and
   (c) Status of secondary buffer stocks of High-Speed Diesel (HSD) across Northern agrarian states ahead of the post-monsoon harvest season.

The Ministry is requested to formulate authenticated statistical replies, annexures, and background briefing notes through the Petroleum Planning & Analysis Cell (PPAC) by 20 August 2026 positively.
